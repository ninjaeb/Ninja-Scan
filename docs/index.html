Capture

Every page, cropped straight and read on the spot

Point the camera at a document and edges are detected, corrected, and cleaned up automatically — shadows and stains removed, up to 50 pages per scan. Every page is run through on-device text recognition the moment it saves, so search finds a document by what's actually written on it, not just its title.

Folders keep the library organized — add, rename, or delete them any time, and filter by tapping a chip.

Organize

Business cards, tagged like they deserve to be

Scanning a card extracts the name, company, title, phone, email, and address on-device — layout-aware, so a name and job title printed side by side don't get run together. Label contacts with reusable, colored tags — Client, Vendor, Follow-up, whatever fits — create, rename, recolor, or delete a tag any time, and search finds contacts by tag too.

Clean up

Long-press, select, gone

Select several documents at once and share or delete them together in one action — no tapping through each one individually. The same long-press opens rename and delete on a folder chip, reliably, every time.

Protect

A watermark that isn't stuck to the file

Add an optional diagonal watermark and it's stamped on the fly whenever the document is viewed, shared, or exported — the stored file itself stays clean, and the watermark stays editable, or removable, at any time. Turn on Drive backup and scans and contacts upload automatically through Google's narrowest Drive permission — the app can only ever see files it created itself.

Privacy, by design

Nothing to opt out of, because there's nothing collecting

Ninja Scan runs no ad network, no analytics SDK, and no third-party tracker of any kind. There's no data pipeline to opt out of — there simply isn't one.

ON-DEVICE

Document and card text recognition happen locally, through ML Kit's on-device APIs — nothing is uploaded to recognize a word.

OPT-IN ONLY

Google Drive backup is off by default and scoped to drive.file — the app can't see the rest of your Drive, ever.

YOURS TO DELETE

Delete a scan or contact any time in-app; uninstalling removes everything local with it.
