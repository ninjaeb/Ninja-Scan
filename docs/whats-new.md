## Website update

**Ninja Scan 1.3 is here** — encrypted backups, biometric lock, and a faster way to build ID card pages.

- **Encrypted Google Drive backups.** Every scan, business card, and backup file is now encrypted before it leaves your device, protected by a private recovery key that only you hold — not stored on our servers or Google's.
- **Unlock with your fingerprint or face.** Ninja Scan can now ask for a quick biometric check when it opens, on any device that already has one set up. Optional, and toggleable from the About screen.
- **Combine a card's front and back into one ID card.** Open any scanned document, select two pages, and combine them into a single, properly formatted ID card layout — without losing the rest of the document.
- **Faster business card tagging.** Add a tag to a business card straight from its menu, no need to open the full editor.
- **Business card editor refresh.** Save and Save to Contacts are now available as buttons at the bottom of the screen too.
- **More reliable backup restore.** Restoring on a new device now reliably brings back your business cards, folders, and tags — and folders keep their colors.
- **Bug fixes:** the Documents tab in the About and Cards screens now always takes you to Documents, business cards correctly show their Google Drive backup status, and the status bar clock is no longer invisible in light mode.
